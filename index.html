What I bring that a typical candidate doesn't

The gap between a marketing manager and an engineer is usually billed as a weakness. In a small or mid-size company it's an unfair advantage, because the person who can change the thing and measure the change is worth more than either hire.

I can hold both ends of the loop

A conversion problem gets diagnosed in the funnel model, not guessed at. If the fix is a landing page change I can ship it. If it's a tracking gap I can instrument it. If it's a creative problem I can brief it. Most agencies hand you a deck instead.

Constraint-driven, which means budget-proof

Everything I've built ran on one ~Rp100k/month box with no extra spend for two months. That trained me to get results out of constrained budgets — which is the actual condition of most marketing jobs, regardless of what the JD says.

AI is an operating model, not a slide

26 million output tokens of agentic development produced a working production system with documentation, runbooks, and disaster recovery. I don't believe AI works — I have the running instance to show you.
