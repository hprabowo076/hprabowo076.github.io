Self-hosting a business stack with AI agents

2025 – present · in production · budget VPS, no other recurring spend

The one-line version: I replaced a spreadsheet-and-no-code stack with a self-hosted production system, built with AI coding agents, with scheduled backups and a tested restore runbook.

Challenge

The reason this exists is a marketing problem. Order flow and customer communication for inbound web and WhatsApp leads were manual, the data lived in Google Sheets, AppSheet and scattered forms, and nobody could get a reliable number without asking me. Two no-code backends I tried first did not fit the workflow. The question became: can I replace the whole stack with something self-hosted, maintainable and cheap, without adding recurring vendors?

Solution

A purpose-built application on a budget VPS, designed around one rule: self-host first, pay second. I built it with AI coding agents and kept the architecture simple enough to run and restore by myself.

LayerTechnologyRole
EdgeReverse proxySingle entry point for the apps
ApplicationTypeScriptInternal operations app: lead capture, sales-rep assignment, dashboards
DataPostgreSQLApplication and automation data
Automationn8nWebhook-driven workflows, self-hosted instead of Zapier
MessagingWhatsApp automationCustomer follow-up from the same system
OperationsMonitoring, scheduled backupsAlerts, and a written, tested restore runbook

The setup is reproducible from a repository rather than from memory: installer, backup and restore scripts, and a drift check that reports through the same alert channel as backup failures. A restore path you have not tested is a wish, not a plan, so the runbook is exercised, not just written.

What the agentic part looked like

The AI did not replace engineering judgment. It replaced the throughput ceiling. The hard parts were not the code. They were deciding what to delete, choosing boring technology that I could operate alone, and knowing when to remove a fragile dependency instead of debugging it again.

The transferable lesson: agentic development moves the bottleneck from typing to architecture and judgment. The sessions where I lost time were never the ones where the model was slow. They were the ones where I had not decided what I wanted yet.

Result

The system has been in production since 2025. Inbound leads are captured and assigned to sales reps automatically, customer messaging runs from the same system, and the team reads daily dashboards computed from it. Reporting no longer depends on asking me for a number.
