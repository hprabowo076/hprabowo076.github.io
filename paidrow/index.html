The problem nobody tells small advertisers

Google and Meta optimize toward whatever you report as a conversion. If that's a form fill or a "conversation started", they find you more people who fill forms and start chats, not people who pay. When sales close by hand, the platforms never see the sale.

The fix is called offline conversion tracking. Big companies wire it into Salesforce. Everyone else gives up because it means click IDs, hashing, two different APIs and fiddly upload formats.

On a real lead-gen account, Google leads reached an order more often than Meta leads (17% vs 13%), but turned into paying customers at almost the same rate (9.8% vs 9.5%). Judging by orders alone would have moved budget the wrong way.

From the founder's own work running Google and Meta Ads for a consumer service business.

How it works

1

Capture the click

A one-line script on your landing page remembers which ad brought the visitor and adds a short Ref code to your WhatsApp button's message.

2

Mark the sale

Type the Ref into the Sheet and the campaign fills in. When they pay, set Status to Paid and enter the amount. That's the whole daily job.

3

Ads learn who pays

Every 15 minutes the sale goes to Meta Conversions API and the Google Ads import tab, with phone and email hashed. Your campaigns can now bid for buyers.

Plus a Report tab: paying customers, revenue and cost per paying customer by campaign. That's the number that should decide your budget.

Questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You copy a Sheet, click a menu item, paste two IDs and one line on your website. The guide shows every click. If you'd rather not, pick the done-for-you setup.

Is my customer data safe?

It never leaves your Google account except to go to Meta and Google, hashed with SHA-256 as both platforms require. PaidRow has no server, and we never see your data.

Does it work with WhatsApp Business (the free app)?

Yes. The click script adds a Ref code to the message customers send you, and you type it into the Sheet. You don't need the WhatsApp Business API.

Why not just use Zapier?

You can, but you'd pay monthly, build the hashing and formatting yourself, and still have no cost-per-paying-customer report. PaidRow is one price, done.

Which countries and currencies?

Any. You set your currency and calling code once in Settings.
