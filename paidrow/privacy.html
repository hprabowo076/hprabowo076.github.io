Privacy

PaidRow is a Google Sheet template with a script that runs inside the buyer's own Google account. Tomato Roll operates no server for PaidRow and does not receive, store or process any lead, customer or ad data that buyers put in their Sheet.

The script sends data only where the buyer configures it: Meta's Conversions API, and a tab that Google Ads reads. Phone numbers and emails are hashed with SHA-256 before being sent or written to that tab. Buyers are responsible for having a lawful basis and a privacy notice for sharing hashed contact data with advertising platforms.

When you buy, our checkout provider processes your payment and gives us your name and email so we can deliver the product and support you. We don't sell or share that information.

This website uses no analytics or tracking cookies.
