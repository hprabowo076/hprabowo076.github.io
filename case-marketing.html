Budget ownership

I have managed an annual media budget of Rp1.2 billion — roughly Rp100 million a month across paid social, paid search, and supporting channels — sustained above 1150% return. The number that actually matters is not the ROAS, it is what happened to the unit economics underneath it.

Margin is where marketing either creates value or hides it. I worked with the CEO, operations, and finance leads to develop the products, the KPIs, and the pricing that turned a decent return into a business that could take a bigger budget next quarter. Unit contribution margin went up as much as 3×.

From domestic to multi-market

I built and ran the company's overseas presence — singapore.baniaqiqah.com and international.baniaqiqah.com — including the launch of five new product lines in three months with a small team. That is a product-launch problem wearing a marketing hat: positioning, creative, landing pages, feed structure, and analytics across a new market.

The reporting layer

Early on I built the daily recap and reporting system for management and the marketing team from scratch — GA4, Meta Ads Manager, Google Ads, and a dashboard that answered the question people actually asked ("what do we do tomorrow?") rather than the question the channel wanted to answer ("what did the channel do?").

A dashboard nobody opens is a report. This one drives the daily media and creative decisions, and it was written to be read by people who do not work in marketing.
