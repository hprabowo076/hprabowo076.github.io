Most ad accounts are judged on what the platform reports. I judge mine on paid invoices, and I built the CRM and tracking that make that possible.

Starting point

In January 2022 I joined Bani Aqiqah, a consumer food-service brand, as Digital Marketing Executive and built the digital marketing function from zero: Google and Meta ad accounts, the WordPress site and landing pages, GA4 tracking, A/B testing and daily management reporting. I was promoted to Marketing Manager in June 2022.

What I own now

The whole acquisition function: paid social, paid search, SEO, conversion optimization, content, and the reporting layer that connects them. That includes around Rp1 billion a year in Google and Meta media and a standing 3–5 person marketing team (social media, content, marketing support and sales/CS). I judge budget on paid invoices reconciled to the CRM I built, and the daily dashboard the team and management read runs on the same data.

I worked with the CEO, operations and finance leads on the products, the KPIs and the pricing. Unit contribution margin rose up to 3× after the new product lines and pricing, so the business could take a bigger budget the next quarter.

Results

  • 19,000+ CRM-recorded leads, about 10% of them paying customers.
  • Paid-invoice revenue up 84% year on year in Q3 2026 (one quarter; see the measurement case for the full picture).
  • Five product lines launched in three months with a three-person team.
  • Singapore and international storefronts (singapore.baniaqiqah.com and international.baniaqiqah.com) built and run by me: positioning, creative, landing pages, feed structure and analytics for a new market.

What I learned

Owning spend, pricing and reporting together meant budget and margin were decided on the same numbers.
